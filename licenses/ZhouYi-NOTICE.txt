Changes: structured extraction of judgments, six bottom-first lines, Tuan and Image passages; separate Qian/Kun special passages; separation of alternate-reading annotations; removal of HTML and a redundant judgment title prefix. Original characters and punctuation are otherwise retained. No automatic prediction or scholarly critical-edition claim is included. Application code, fonts and other dependencies have separate licenses.

Modern editorial readings are provided separately in hexagram-readings.json, line-readings-*.json and LineReading.swift. They are AI-assisted original summaries prepared for YaoJi, not ancient quotations or translations by the named classical authors. Ancient commentary references include Zhu Xi's Zhouyi Benyi and Wang Bi / Kong Yingda's Zhouyi Zhengyi. The editorial corpus and its source-text adaptations are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms above; this does not change the licenses of the application code or other assets.
